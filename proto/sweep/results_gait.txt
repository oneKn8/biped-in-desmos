Columns. 20 s two-in-a-row pass: seeds whose mean row passed the 20 s exam twice in a row within 100k training ticks
(for the gated configs the exam also requires asymmetry <= 0.15 and mean |torso angle| <= 8 deg). Median ticks (IQR):
training ticks before the second passing exam, a seed that never passed counts as +inf. Desmos min: median ticks
including that exam / 22 steps per s / 60 (rate measured for 97 bodies; the gait accumulators add per-step work that has
not been timed in Desmos). Ref 20 s / 60 s bar: reference re-check of the walkers that passed. Asymmetry = |left - right
single-support time| / (left + right); mean abs torso = mean |torso angle| while upright (0 = upright); signed torso and
torso std (the pitch swing) over the same 20 s reference rollout. All gait numbers are medians over the walkers.

Knob names (long_train.py): lean = w_lean (penalty on sum dt * torso angle^2), asym = w_asym, air = w_air (default 3),
airfree = air_free (default 0.15), anti = anti_scale (noise antisymmetric part scale), decay = sym_decay (pull of theta
to its mirror-symmetric part per update), cap = v_cap; a 2 suffix means the value used from the first passing episode
on (lean2, air2, anti2, decay2, airfree2, nu2, a2); exam asym / exam torso = bar_asym / bar_lean on the 20 s exams.

Notes.
* The gaits are chaotic: for one walker the fast trainer (fastsim, batched) and ../sim.py differ by 8.5e-15 m after
  1 s, 8.9e-8 m after 5 s and 2.8 m after 15 s (e-folding about 0.25 s); fastsim's own result for a row also depends on
  the batch it runs in (floating-point summation order). So a training exam pass is a statement about one trajectory.
  The one walker here whose two training exams did not reproduce in the reference (G_homo05_asymbar seed 7) is such a
  case; the reference column and the jittered rates are the honest numbers.
* G_anti0s_air8_free0 was stopped early (9k ticks) on the evidence of its two siblings (same idea, 1/8 at ~36-44k).
* Stopped configs were killed when their median seed could no longer pass within 1.5x the adopted cost, or when
  their mean rows showed the failure the row describes (airborne > 0.25, limp unchanged, exams falling).

Recommended trade-off: "symmetrized walk + gait exam" (config G_homo05_l3_bar; walkers in long_walkers/P96_symmetrized_gait-exam)
Before the first pass it is exactly the adopted recipe; three things switch on at the first passing episode (the flag
P_e the graph already keeps for nu), and the exam gets two gait tests.
  Unchanged: 97 bodies (48 antithetic directions + mean row), top 24; nu 0.12 -> 0.08 (noise from the episode after
  the first pass), alpha 0.15 -> 0.08 (from the update right after it); 5 s chained windows (300 steps); a 20 s exam
  from rest (1200 steps) after 2 passing windows in a row, cut when the mean row falls; a failed exam goes back to
  windows from rest; stop after 2 passing exams in a row; bar: >= 0.8 m/s, no fall, >= 3 alternating single-support
  phases per foot per 5 s, both feet up < 25% of steps.
  Fitness, episodes after the first pass (the passing episode itself is scored the old way):
      F = dist - 8 * max(0, air - 0.15 * alive) - 3 * L2,   L2 = sum over steps while upright of dt * theta^2
      theta = torso angle in radians, atan2(head.x - hip.x, head.y - hip.y), the policy's first input, after the step;
      "while upright" multiplies by the alive flag after the step, as F_air does. Before the first pass:
      F = dist - 3 * max(0, air - 0.15 * alive) (unchanged).
  Noise, from the episode after the first pass: D' = 0.5 * (D + s * D[I]), used in place of D both in the bodies'
      parameters v(p) and in the update w_n. Parameter p = 13 a + o (a = 0 left hip, 1 left knee, 2 right hip,
      3 right knee; o = the 13 inputs in sim.observe order). Its mirror partner is pi(p) = 13 ((a + 2) mod 4) + mu(o),
      mu = (0, 1, 4, 5, 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 8, 10, 11, 12), with sign s = -1 for o = 10, 11 (clock sin, cos), else +1.
      In gen_desmos's layout D[n_d p + j] this is one constant index list I[n_d p + j] = n_d pi(p) + j and one sign
      list, 52 * 48 = 2496 long each (one gather per episode).
  Pull to symmetry, from the update right after the first pass (the same switch as alpha):
      w_0 <- w_n - 0.025 * (w_n - s * w_n[pi])   (theta <- 0.975 theta + 0.025 mirror(theta); a 52-long gather)
      With the noise above this shrinks the antisymmetric part of theta by 5% per update and never re-explores it.
  Exam bar (exams only; windows keep the old bar), on the mean row, in addition to the old tests:
      |S_L - S_R| <= 0.15 (S_L + S_R)   and   A_theta <= (8 pi / 180) * alive
      S_L = sum dt * [left foot y < 0.001] * [right foot y >= 0.001] while upright, S_R the mirror, A_theta =
      sum dt * |theta| while upright.
  New per-episode state: L2 per body (97 numbers) and S_L, S_R, A_theta for the mean row (3 numbers, or per-body
  lists for simplicity), reset with F_d / F_a / F_air and updated in every unrolled physics step like F_air.
  Numpy knobs (long_train.py): w_lean2=3, w_air2=8, anti_scale2=0, sym_decay2=0.05, bar_asym=0.15, bar_lean=8,
  gait_stages=[false, true] on top of config_L_chain2_cut_dec_P96_f2.json, budget 100k ticks.
  What it costs (24 seeds): 14/24 seeds pass within 100k ticks (adopted: 22/24); median 77.7k ticks (2.2x the
  adopted 35.9k), about 60 Desmos minutes at 22 steps/s, IQR 62.8k-inf; first 8 seeds alone 6/8 at 64.5k, next 16
  8/16. What it buys (14 walkers, reference): asymmetry 0.014 (max 0.04, adopted 0.366), mean |torso| 7.1 deg (max
  8.0, adopted 26.6), torso std 8.2 deg (adopted 25.2), airborne 1% (adopted 14%), 1.23 m/s, 20 s 14/14, 60 s 13/14
  (adopted 10/22), jittered-start 20 s / 60 s pass rates 0.94 / 0.83 (adopted 0.65 / 0.40).
